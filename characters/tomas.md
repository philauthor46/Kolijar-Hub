# Tomas

**Role:** Kiris's apprentice
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Tomas is the apprentice to Kiris, learning the craft of blacksmithing while being drawn into the larger events surrounding the court. His youth and training place him on the edges of the unfolding conflict, where he is still finding his place.
