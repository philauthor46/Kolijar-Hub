# Parell Lome

**Role:** King of Harenjun
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Parell Lome rules Harenjun with authority and a measured hand, balancing diplomacy and power as one of the neighboring crowns most closely tied to Kreturia's fate. His kingdom's fortunes are shaped by the political tensions surrounding Serafina's reign.
