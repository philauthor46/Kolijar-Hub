# Merinda

**Role:** Princess of Harenjun
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Merinda is a member of Harenjun's royal family, carrying the grace and expectations of a princess in a court that is never far from conflict. Her place in the political web around Kreturia makes her more than a distant noble figure.
