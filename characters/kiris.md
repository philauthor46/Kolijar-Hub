# Kiris

**Role:** Royal blacksmith
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Kiris is the royal blacksmith, a skilled maker of steel and a practical presence in the court. His work supports the kingdom's defenses and his skill makes him an essential figure in the life of Kreturia.
