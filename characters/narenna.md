# Narenna

**Role:** Princess of Harenjun
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Narenna is part of Harenjun's royal line, where her life is shaped by duty, alliances, and the growing unrest between the kingdoms. Her presence reflects the political pressure that hangs over Serafina's uncertain future.
