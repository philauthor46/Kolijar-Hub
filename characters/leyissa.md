# Leyissa

**Role:** Loyal friend of the Harenjun royal family
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Leyissa is a loyal and trusted friend to the royal family of Harenjun, known for her steadiness and devotion. Her presence reflects the strong personal ties that bind the courts together in a time of uneasy alliances.
