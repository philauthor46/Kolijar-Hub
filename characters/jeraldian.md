# Jeraldian

**Role:** Prince of Lustralia
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Jeraldian is a prince of Lustralia whose life is shaped by the expectations of a ruling house and the strategic weight of his kingdom. He stands at the edge of a larger political story that begins to move around Serafina's fate.
