# Janto

**Role:** King of Lustralia
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Janto rules Lustralia as a monarch whose authority is tied to the region's strength and stability. His role in the wider kingdom politics makes him a figure of consequence in the struggle unfolding around Kreturia.
