# Maris Lome

**Role:** Queen of Harenjun
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Maris Lome is the queen of Harenjun, a poised and influential ruler whose presence carries weight in the wider politics of the kingdoms. She stands beside her husband as a key voice in the delicate balance of power around Kreturia.
