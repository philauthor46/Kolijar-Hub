# Reyira

**Role:** Queen of Kreturia
**First appears:** [Seat of Embers]({{ "/books/seat-of-embers/" | relative_url }})

Reyira is the queen of Kreturia, a central figure in the kingdom's historical and political story. Her place in the royal lineage gives her significance in the emotional and political weight that shapes Serafina's path.
