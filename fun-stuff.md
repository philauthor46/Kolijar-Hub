# Fun Stuff

A little corner for the lighter side of Kolijar: theories, trivia, hidden details, and the sort of things fans keep debating over tea.

## Fan theories

- The Crystal may not only choose a Guardian — it may also be choosing a future ruler through the people who keep protecting it.
- Serafina's strongest power may not be magic at all, but her ability to make people choose courage over fear.
- Pteryll's watchfulness might be a sign that he knows more about the Crystal than he admits.
- The founding prophecy may have been written in a way that was meant to be misunderstood until the right people were ready to hear it.

## Trivia and hidden details

- Teyerun is more than just a village: it is the living reminder that the kingdom's heart is not always in the castle.
- The three houses all claim different strengths: Curatal for resilience, Lome for craft and culture, Desallez for order and endurance.
- The phrase "the Heart alone shall command its fire" hints that emotional truth matters as much as raw magic.
- Several key moments in Book One revolve around the idea that food, memory, and family are as important as swords.

## Fun facts

- No one in the royal families is ever fully safe from a difficult truth.
- Marigal's practical wisdom is perhaps the most dangerous weapon in the entire series.
- Lizera's comment about changing the rules is a perfect example of why she is such a quietly brilliant character.
- The kingdom's greatest danger is not always the outside enemy — sometimes it is the old, comfortable rule that refuses to change.
