# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Kolijar** (KO-LEE-JAR) | The island and setting of the series |
| **Kreturia** | Kingdom ruled by House Curatal |
| **Harenjun** | Kingdom ruled by House Lome |
| **Lustralia** (Lus-TRAH-lia) | Kingdom ruled by House Desallez |
| **Trinity Accord** | Ancient mutual defence pact between the three houses |
| **Drivaran** | Kreturia's castle and seat of power |
| **Teyerun** | Village at the foot of Drivaran |
| **Verekel** | Capital city of Harenjun |
| **Pokaril** | Capital city of Lustralia |
| **Teverin** | Lustralia's coastline |
| **Petulira Mountains** | Mountain range and landmark of the island |
| **The Crystal** | The island's beating heart and source of all power |
| **First Order of Sorcery** | Order of sorcerers and knights, guardians of the crystal |

*Keep adding terms. Alphabetical order helps readers.*
