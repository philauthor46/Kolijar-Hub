# Quotes

A small collection worth returning to.

## Book One: Seat of Embers

> "The Guardian shall protect the flame, but the Heart alone shall command its fire."
— *The founding prophecy of the Crystal*

> "A kingdom is not its castle. It is not its throne. It is not its crown. A kingdom is its people."
— *Serafina, reclaiming Teyerun*

> "Leyanna took your choices from you. I won't."
— *Serafina, freeing the Dark bringers*

> "Because it kept you alive long enough for me to meet you twice."
— *Pteryll, to Serafina*

> "A king who eats while his people starve is no king at all."
— *King Herrellus, remembered by Joris*

> "No Queen ever saved a kingdom on an empty stomach."
— *Marigal*

> "I think you've just realised how much you've already lost."
— *Pteryll, to Leyanna*

> "You looked at me and saw the Heart. But my father saw me... Because he loved me."
— *Serafina, to Leyanna*

> "The Herallum Box... was an excellent teacher."
— *Leyanna, upon her return*

> "You're still my son... whatever happens out there, come back."
— *King Janto, to Jeraldian*

> "Because somebody should have."
— *Serafina, on why she helps the freed Dark bringers*

> "The Guardian does not choose the Crystal. The Crystal chooses its Guardian."
— *Pteryll, on the nature of the Crystal*

> "A king who eats while his people starve is no king at all... but you're still my son."
— *Janto, echoing his old friend while forgiving his own*

> "Then they'll have to change the rules."
— *Lizera, as a girl, on becoming a knight*
