# Philip A. Hughes

Author of The Kolijar Chronicles

Philip A. Hughes is the creator of The Kolijar Chronicles, a fantasy series shaped by royal intrigue, ancient magic, and the difficult choices that come with power.

## About the series

The world of Kolijar began as a place to explore what happens when kingdoms are built on old loyalties, fractured trust, and a crystal that seems to choose its own destiny. The stories that follow are rooted in loyalty, loss, courage, and the messy realities of leadership.

## Writing focus

Philip writes with a love for:

- strong emotional stakes
- character-driven fantasy
- morally complex decisions
- richly layered worldbuilding
- stories where the heart of the realm matters as much as the magic

## Why Kolijar?

Kolijar is a world where the people matter as much as the prophecy. Every kingdom, every house, and every choice carries weight. The aim is to make the setting feel lived in — not just beautiful, but dangerous, personal, and full of history.
