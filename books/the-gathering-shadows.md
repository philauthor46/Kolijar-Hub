# The Gathering Shadows

*The Kolijar Chronicles, Book Two*

The crown sits heavier than the crystal ever did.

Queen Serafina Curatal has reclaimed Kreturia, but a throne won through fire is not the same as a throne held in peace. Her people whisper doubts her father never had to face, and the crystal's power — the very gift meant to protect her kingdom — begins to reveal secrets Serafina isn't ready to carry.

Leyanna is not gone. Weakened but unbroken, she gathers her strength in the shadows beyond Kolijar's borders, and something far more dangerous stirs with her.

Into the chaos comes Peris Korvil — a hunter with no crown to serve and no mercy to spare for the woman who shattered his family. His path collides with Serafina's, and what begins as rivalry hardens into an uneasy alliance neither of them wanted.

As old betrayals resurface and new ones take root within her own court, Serafina must learn to wield a power she doesn't yet understand — before the shadows gathering around her kingdom, and within it, tear everything she's rebuilt back down to ash.
