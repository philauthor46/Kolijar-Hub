# Echoes of Embers

*The Kolijar Chronicles, Book Three*

The crystal remembers what she has yet to learn.

The war is over, but the reckoning has only begun. Queen Serafina Curatal carries a power she still doesn't fully understand — and a truth about its origin that could unmake her sense of who she is.

Leyanna is gone, but what used her is not. Kojarin, the darkness that gave her power and abandoned her the moment she stopped being useful, has found a new vessel closer to home — and a new, more dangerous purpose.

With Pteryll's long-buried secret finally spoken aloud, and the people closest to her tested one by one, Serafina must find a way to fight back that Kojarin has never accounted for — not fear, not love, not the parts of herself she has spent years trying to bury.

Some victories cost everything. Some truths change who you thought you were. And some love is worth every ember it costs to keep alight.
