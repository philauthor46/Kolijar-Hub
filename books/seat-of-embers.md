# Seat of Embers

*The Kolijar Chronicles, Book One*

> A Kingdom Lost. A Crown Reclaimed.
> Some thrones must be won twice.

Queen Serafina's rule over Kreturia collapses when a vengeful witch attacks Kolijar seeking an ancient relic. Pteryll Jurin, a trusted ally and Knight of the First Order of Sorcery, helps her confront the threat.

Years earlier, Jurin had imprisoned Leyanna, whose thirst for power almost cost the kingdom the Kolijar crystal, the island's beating heart and the source of all power across the three kingdoms.

Together they embark on a dangerous journey as Serafina learns harsh truths, discovers betrayals from those closest to her, and grapples with her own personal demons.
